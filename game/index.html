EMBER COURT

A duel of cunning cards against the Ash Duchess

You sit at the Ember Court with 20 life. Each round you and the Duchess each play one card face down, then reveal. The stronger card wins the trick and wounds the loser. Reduce her to 0 life — or hold more life after 12 rounds — to take the Court.

Ember is your cost. Every card costs ember to play; you gain ember each round (3, then +1 a round, up to 6). Unspent ember carries over.
Elements matter. Flame beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Flame — a winning element strikes at +2 power. Gale bows to none.
Win the trick, wound the foe. Damage equals the power gap. A tie wounds you both for 1. Two tricks in a row earns Court Favor: +1 ember every round.
Cards carry tricks of their own: draw extra cards, heal, raise a shield, or siphon the Duchess's ember.
Keys: 1–5 play a card  ·  M mulligan (once a game)  ·  Enter continue  ·  N new game  ·  S sound
An original game — no borrowed cards, characters or art.

VICTORY

The Court is yours.

HOW TO PLAY

Your turn: click a card in your hand (or press 1–5). If it costs more ember than you hold, it stays greyed out — pick another.

The clash: both cards turn over at once. Power + element bonus decides it. The winner deals damage equal to the gap; the loser loses that much life. A tie deals 1 damage to each side.

After the clash: the winner banks +1 ember and both sides draw back up to 5 cards. Shields block damage first; healing and siphons fire the moment a card is played.

Winning: drop the Duchess to 0 life, or finish 12 rounds ahead on life. Lose all your life and the Court keeps your seat.
